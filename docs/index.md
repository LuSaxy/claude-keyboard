<section class="clawd-specs" aria-label="What it takes">
<dl>
  <div>
    <dt>Board</dt>
    <dd><strong>XIAO nRF52840 Sense</strong> or plain XIAO, on a 2-layer 59.8 × 36 mm PCB. Not the Plus.</dd>
  </div>
  <div>
    <dt>Soldering</dt>
    <dd>XIAO castellations, 0603/0805 passives, two SMD slide and side switches. Or have the board assembled.</dd>
  </div>
  <div>
    <dt>Printing</dt>
    <dd>Two shells, eye covers, USB bezel and battery plate. PLA or PETG; two materials optional.</dd>
  </div>
  <div>
    <dt>Tools</dt>
    <dd>Fine-tip iron, flux, multimeter (battery polarity), USB-C data cable.</dd>
  </div>
  <div>
    <dt>Out of the box</dt>
    <dd>Escape · Enter on the keys, Shift+Tab · Tab on the shoulders. Lift to wake, double tap and tilt on the Sense.</dd>
  </div>
  <div>
    <dt>Status</dt>
    <dd>V2 prototype: rev A boards built and typing. Slide-switch fit and antenna keepout still unverified.</dd>
  </div>
</dl>
<p class="note">Prices depend on where you buy and how many boards you order; the <a href="./bom">parts list</a> has every part with an LCSC number and link.</p>
</section>
